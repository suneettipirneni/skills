# Proposition, proof and next action

Layer: **organisms** · Route: `marketing-region`

**Use when:** A visitor needs to understand value and decide whether to continue.

**Avoid when:** Decoration substitutes for evidence or a task entry already explains itself.

## Decision and composition

Every section should answer a real uncertainty: relevance, mechanism, credibility, fit, cost or next step. Vary composition when the content role changes, not on an arbitrary alternating-card schedule.

Compose a concrete proposition, meaningful image/demo when useful, factual proof and an appropriate action. Distinguish evidence from illustration. A testimonial needs real provenance; decorative avatars are not customers.

## Behavior and adaptation

Ensure the narrative survives narrow reordering, unavailable images and reduced motion. Repeated actions must lead to the stated next step. Preserve distinctive art direction while maintaining readable labels and operable controls.

## Evidence and repair

Check claim/evidence adjacency and heading wraps. Inspect whether each section adds information. Numeric credibility claims require actual data and sourcing; a design audit cannot create them.

**Worked decision:** A workflow product shows the before/after task with an actual example instead of four identical “seamless, powerful, intuitive” feature cards.

**Source boundary:** This is original composition synthesis. Practitioner principles and evidence boundaries are documented in the main research ledger.
