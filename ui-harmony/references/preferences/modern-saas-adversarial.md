# Adversarial review: modern SaaS preference, 2026-11-15

Verdict: useful as an explicitly authored preference rubric with mandatory independent functional gates. The implemented scope and evidence restrictions are materially better than a single beauty score. The independently reproduced comparison/integration gaps were repaired and retested. No remaining blocking arithmetic or validation defect was found in the tested paths. This is not an empirical validation of the weights or evidence that the same reviewer will reproduce every ordinal judgment.

## Independent research and design objections

Research and source boundaries are in [independent research](modern-saas-adversarial-research.md): 12 primary articles/system references, including Linear's 2024 and 2026 team accounts, Vercel Geist, Radix, Intercom, NN/g, Rauno Freiberg and Emil Kowalski. The reviewed parent ledger adds established practitioners and resilient-layout sources. Sources support directions and tradeoffs, not the 20/20/15/15/15/10/5 weights.

The important distinction is controlled task hierarchy and density, not fewer visible elements. The final guide explicitly preserves useful table density, error/status/data color, labels, domain vocabulary, warm brands, meaningful layer boundaries and immediate repeated interactions. It permits deliberate neutrality without requiring novelty. It warns against triple penalizing one decorative issue across hierarchy, containment and color. Marketing and hybrid typography now have explicit region-relative interpretation.

Examples of bad scoring incentives that the prose now resists: a tiny gray clone; a sparse dashboard missing essential actions; an icon-only rail requiring repeated hover; a flattening of dialogs until boundaries disappear; repeated spring animation in frequent commands; stripping red from a real error; forcing a serif marketing region into compact app typography. These are conceptual calibration cases, not experimentally verified outcomes.

## Actual tool probes and repairs

The independent reviewer ran a local scoring probe script during development. The [recorded probe output](../../assets/examples/saas/adversarial-probes.json) is bundled; inputs and assertions are deliberately synthetic, not fabricated user-study evidence. Probes cover all-3 arithmetic, incomplete criteria, failed and unknown gates, static evidence, malformed and nonfinite ratings, malformed container types, context changes, reviewer changes, failed high scorers and extra metadata.

Observed correct behavior:

- All criteria rated 3 produce 75; missing one rating withholds total rather than renormalizing.
- A perfect 100 with failed legibility remains blocked; unknown gates are provisional.
- Static all-pass claims are rejected; static unknown gates are preserved.
- Invalid ratings and malformed JSON-shaped structures return diagnostics without exceptions in tested cases.
- Authored weight, contribution, result and recommendation metadata do not overwrite canonical calculations.
- Different declared task, audience, route, surface, fixture, viewport, theme, state, evidence mode, scope and reviewer refuse comparison. Both current version and passing scoped gates are needed.

Actual defects reproduced and repaired:

1. Audience originally was not compared; a trained-SRE review and first-time-customer review could compare. Audience is now required and comparison-bound.
2. A full review could describe mobile checkout and an unresolved accessibility failure while its embedded style review described another dashboard with passing gates. Enclosing task/audience/scope and known failures now constrain the embedded assessment.
3. The rejected full review initially still emitted a nested comparison-eligible preference result. Final CLI output now emits `invalid-enclosing-review`, score null, exit 2.
4. Saved scores originally omitted reviewer provenance. Final output retains it.

Final actual CLI evidence: [CLI evidence](../../assets/examples/saas/adversarial-cli.json). All 38 repository tests passed during this review; the later output/provenance repair was additionally checked directly through the CLI. No claim is made here that these tests verify the truth of human-authored evidence.

## Remaining limits and application advice

- Ratings and weights remain authored ordinal policy. A one-point aggregate change is arithmetic, not statistically meaningful improvement.
- The validator establishes structural completeness, not truth. Changing evidenceMode to rendered or supplying confident prose cannot establish a real interaction test.
- A fixed total can hide criterion tradeoffs; inspect the breakdown and preserved strengths. Weighted style gaps are not failure severity.
- Compare product and marketing regions separately when their roles differ. Modern SaaS is a user preference, not a reason to call excellent public-service, expressive or editorial designs poor UX.
- A real multi-reviewer reliability exercise and task-tested matched alternatives would be needed to establish repeatability and practical predictive value. They have not been performed in this iteration.
